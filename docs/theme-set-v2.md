# Invitation theme set v2 — research output and build specs

Produced by a 17-agent research workflow: market sweep (Egyptian/Gulf market, global
platforms, 2025-26 trends, Arabic typography, regional motifs, mobile motion craft,
critique of the existing four, bilingual/a11y constraints) -> positioning brief -> four
independent concept panels -> three judge lenses -> canonical set.
55 concepts proposed, 12 selected. All palettes verified against WCAG AA.

**Status.** Nine of the twelve (`hadiqa`, `iwan`, `ghouroub`, `mashrabiya`, `qandeel`, `khayamiya`, `netiga`, `zarf`, `rizma`) are built and live with their Cover + Invitation components registered in `src/themes/components.ts` and `listed: true` in `src/themes/registry.ts`. The legacy four (`classic`, `modern`, `floral`, `midnight`) remain renderable with `listed: false`.

**Three were built and then cut: `makhmal`, `sijill`, `lawh`.** Their components, registry entries and eight now-orphaned typefaces were deleted outright rather than retired to `listed: false`, which the code is otherwise strict about. That is safe only because none of the three ever reached `main` — they were built and cut on the same unmerged branch, so no invitation in the database can carry one of those ids. The rule still holds for everything that has shipped. Their specs below are kept as a record and marked; nothing renders them.

---

## Set rationale

TWELVE THEMES, CHOSEN FOR COVERAGE NOT FOR SCORE.

Three judges produced overlapping but not identical top tens. I did not take the union. I took the smallest set that (a) puts one theme in every segment × occasion × ground cell the positioning brief names, (b) gives each theme a structural device no other theme in the set — including the four existing ones — uses, and (c) survives a mid-range Android. Several 8-scoring concepts were cut for redundancy; two 6-scoring concepts were kept because nothing else covers what they cover.

THE TWELVE STRUCTURAL AXES (the differentiation rule, satisfied by construction). Each theme owns exactly one and no other theme may borrow it: مشربية aperture (content in holes cut through a full-bleed screen) · الإيوان frame-as-measure (an arch whose legs define and taper the reading width) · قنديل suspension and z-depth (unequal plumb lines on three parallax planes) · مخمل continuous surface (one uninterrupted material, seams instead of rules) · خيامية field stack (discrete saturated cloths, sewn seams, zero page margin) · غروب horizon (one moving line, content flipping above and below it) · النتيجة dated leaves (a bound pad you tear) · السجل ruled table (a strict baseline pitch with label and value columns) · الظرف fold grid (one sheet creased into three panels at content boundaries) · حديقة off-centre column with a live ornament gutter (ornament as an index of sections) · الرزمة discrete objects (five overlapping cards, five different measures, inverted panel) · اللوح monolith (one continuous block, zero section breaks). None of these is classic's bordered panel, modern's centred column of hairlines, floral's rounded panel with corner sprigs, or midnight's centred column with an invisible spine.

TWO ARE REPLACEMENTS, NOT ADDITIONS — stated so the call can be made deliberately. قنديل is midnight done properly: same dark-and-gold promise, but the structure is genuinely visible (things hang, at three depths, on lines of unequal length) where midnight's spine sits behind a fully centred column and cannot be seen. Shipping both means selling the same promise twice, one of them badly; the market judge's own warning — that customers will read قنديل as a recolour unless the picker shows the hung structure — is the argument for replacement, not for coexistence. حديقة is floral done honestly: the brief says painterly floral is out of reach and line work is the substitute, and حديقة is that substitute with a real structure attached, so it should take floral's slot rather than sit beside a theme the brief has already retired. The other ten are additions. Nothing here replaces classic — see the note on اللوح below.

WHY THESE AND NOT THE OTHER HIGH SCORERS. شمعة scored 8/6/8 and is cut anyway: it is a dark, gold, evening, one-lit-object card for segment B, and قنديل is already that. Two excellent concepts in one segment in one register means one goes. مشربية and الإيوان both survived a similar test only because I separated them — مشربية's ground is now pale with a deep walnut lattice (light coming through the screen) against الإيوان's warm Cairene stone, and their devices are opposites: one puts content in holes, the other puts a frame around it. ميقات (7/7/7, a genuinely good timeline) is cut because its scroll-drawn vertical line at an inline offset is حديقة's device, and because its running-order content requires a schema change the product does not yet have — a theme that depends on a schema change is the wrong thing to put in a launch set. حنة (8/7/6) is cut because خيامية already owns the henna night and حديقة already owns the drawn line. طيّة (8/6/6) is cut because its four leaves composite at 0.82 alpha, which makes every one of its seven contrast tokens notional — a theme whose colour contract cannot be computed breaks the system — and الرزمة delivers the paper-object idea with opaque cards and real numbers. ختم is folded into الظرف, which the build judge scored three points higher and which places its creases by content boundary so a long custom message cannot break the fold logic.

THE LADDER. Light grounds carry the volume (nine of twelve), because that is what this market buys and what 300 EGP anchors. The top of the ladder is deliberately two objects, قنديل and مخمل, not four: a dark theme that reads as a recolour damages the two that do not. خيامية is the only fully saturated ground in the set and it is correct there — maximalism is the right answer for a henna night and the wrong answer everywhere else.

TYPE. Twelve distinct Arabic display faces and eleven distinct Arabic body faces across twelve themes (Noto Naskh Arabic is the only repeat, as the plain workhorse under مخمل and الظرف). Amiri appears nowhere as a pairing face; Amiri Quran appears in all twelve as the verse face only, which is the correctness rule, not a style choice. Cairo appears nowhere. The register spread runs geometric Kufi (Kufam, Noto Kufi Arabic, Qahiri, Reem Kufi Ink), flat poster display (Rakkas, Lalezar, Zain), manuscript Naskh (Scheherazade New, Ruwudu, Mirza), and contemporary display (Alexandria, El Messiri) — four voices, not one.

CONTRAST. Every ink, muted and accent token in all twelve themes was computed against both its own bg and its own panel; all thirty-six pairs clear 4.5:1, most with two points of headroom. Five judge-flagged tightnesses were fixed by changing the hex, not by arguing: مشربية's whole palette lifted (accent 5.30 → 6.87 on panel), غروب's accent darkened to #93381f (5.41 → 5.95) because its six blended bands mean text sits on grounds that are not tokens, الظرف's muted darkened to #5f5747 (4.88 → 5.60), حديقة's accent darkened to #356044 (5.07 → 5.98), and اللوح's ground warmed from limestone grey to ivory, which raised ink from 10.21 to 12.81 on panel and answered the market judge's "drab" objection in the same move. accentSoft is below 4.5:1 in every light theme by design and is declared ornament-only in each spec, per the house rule that a light-ground theme budgets a decorative accent and a separately darkened text accent.

## The twelve as researched (nine shipped)


### مشربية — Mashrabiya (`mashrabiya`) — spec only

**Segment.** B primary (world-building Cairo bride), A secondary. The بوابة / Gate slot. زفاف, and خطوبة as a colourway.

**Pitch.** The turned-wood window screen of an old Cairo house. The card starts as a screen you cannot see through, and opens by cutting a window in it — after that every part of the invitation sits in its own hole in the lattice, never on a page.

**Palette.** bg `#f7f1e4` · panel `#ebe1cc` · ink `#241c14` · muted `#64492b` · accent `#6f3d16` · accentSoft `#c8a06a` · line `rgba(111, 61, 22, 0.30)` · themeColor `#f7f1e4`

**Contrast.** ink 14.91:1 on bg / 12.93:1 on panel; muted 7.38 / 6.39; accent 7.92 / 6.87. accentSoft 2.15 / 1.86 — decorative only, aria-hidden ornament and rims, never text. FIX GRAFTED: the original palette (bg #f2e7d6, accent #7f4a1c) passed at 5.30 on panel but sat within a hair of الإيوان's warm stone. The ground is lifted to a pale sun-through-the-screen tone and the accent deepened to true walnut, which both separates the two themes at picker size and raises every ratio by roughly 1.5 points.

**Fonts.** AR Kufam / Almarai — EN Marcellus / Spectral. Weights: Kufam 400 and 700 (names at 700; the 500 is never used, no synthetic weights). Almarai 400 and 700. Amiri Quran 400 for the Bismillah and Ar-Rum 21 only — declared as this theme's own verse token at 1.0625rem / 2.0 leading, never inherited. Marcellus 400 (single weight by design). Spectral 400 and 500.

> Kufam is a wide, engineered Kufi whose stem rhythm has the same regular vertical beat as the bobbins in a turned-wood screen — the type and the ornament are built on one grid. Almarai is the flattest neutral Arabic on the list and stays out of the way inside a void where the lattice is already doing the work. Marcellus is inscriptional Roman: flat-topped, low contrast, the same authority as Kufam, so the English card is the same object rather than a translation. Spectral gives the Latin body the warmth Almarai deliberately withholds.


**Structure.** APERTURE — the axis this theme owns. There is no column, no panel and no page frame anywhere. A turned-wood lattice covers the entire viewport at full density, full bleed, edge to edge. Each content block sits inside a clear VOID cut into that screen: a rounded-hexagon opening 300px wide at a 390px viewport, with a 2px bobbin-turned rim in accent, the lattice faded out inside the void by a radial mask so the reading area is clean --inv-bg. Content therefore never sits on a field; it sits in a hole. Consecutive voids alternate which side of the measure they hang from — inline-start, centre, inline-end, inline-start — because a mashrabiya is a wall, not a spine. Void heights differ by content: the names void is 380px tall, the verse void 300px, the date void 240px, the countdown void 170px. Vertical gap between voids is 56px of solid lattice, so the screen is genuinely between the blocks. Usable measure inside a hexagonal void is 244px, NOT the 300px the void looks — every measure token must be authored from that number.


**Ornament.** One bin: turned wood (خرط), three parts and nothing else. (1) THE BOBBIN — a vesica/lens turned on a lathe. viewBox 0 0 16 40; path is two mirrored quadratic curves meeting at points top and bottom with a 6px waist at mid-height, stroke 1.25px currentColor. (2) THE SIX-LOBED ROSETTE — six bobbins radiating at 60° from a common centre, their tips meeting on a 90px radius; the negative space between them is the third part. viewBox 0 0 200 200, strokes 1.5px. HERO: one rosette at 180px behind the names inside the names void, accent at 30% opacity. CONNECTIVE: a run of three bobbins laid end to end horizontally, 64px wide, 1.25px in accent — the only divider in the theme. GROUND: a 22px CSS data-URI tile of the hex-and-bobbin repeat at 8% of accent, tiled full bleed. Every shape is symmetric about both axes, so RTL costs nothing. All SVG aria-hidden, pointer-events-none, currentColor, ids from useId().


**Cover.** CLOSED: an opaque lattice screen edge to edge at full density in walnut — you cannot read through it. The couple's names appear only as a dim silhouette BEHIND the screen: a second copy of the names rendered in the same DOM node at 12% ink under the lattice layer, with no filter and no dependency on a webfont having loaded (the silhouette must be legible in the fallback stack, or the closed card reads as broken while the font swaps). The open control reads افتح الدعوة on a solid accent bar sitting on the lattice, not floating over it. OPEN: the aperture opens. The lattice is one inline SVG whose CSS mask-image is a radial-gradient with a hard stop; that mask element is animated by transform: scale(0) → scale(1.4) from the centre over 900ms ease-out on a single promoted layer, so the screen dissolves outward into a window and the card is revealed as what was always behind it. FIX GRAFTED (build): do NOT animate an SVG <mask> circle — that repaints the masked layer every frame. Use a CSS mask-image radial-gradient on one will-change: transform layer and scale it. FIX GRAFTED (market): the names silhouette is at 12% but the OPEN BUTTON and the event line are at full ink on the lattice, so the closed card never looks like it failed to load. Reduced motion: the aperture is already open at full size, the card cross-fades.


**Card.** 1 NAMES — inside the first void, centred, Kufam 700 at 52px, four lines from the start (both fathers). The 180px rosette sits behind at 30%. Long-name case: عبد الرحمن و ياسمين is allowed to break to three lines inside the 244px measure; the void grows downward, it does not shrink the type. 2 BISMILLAH + VERSE — its own void, inline-start hung, Amiri Quran, Bismillah 2.25rem, verse 1.0625rem / 2.0, source line 11px muted. The lattice tile is suppressed to 0 behind this void: Qur'anic text never sits over a pattern. 3 POETRY — a small centre void, 200px tall, Almarai 400 at 18px in muted, one bobbin run above it. 4 INVITATION LINE — inline-end hung void, Almarai 400 at 17px ink. 5 ROLES — one void holding both, العريس and العروس stacked with a three-bobbin divider between them, labels 11px muted with word-spacing 0.3em (never letter-spacing). 6 PHOTO — fills one hexagonal void exactly via clip-path with a 2px accentSoft rim, reading as the view through the window. NO PHOTO: that same void holds the six-lobed rosette at full 180px in accent — the void is the container and the photo is only one of two things that can live in it, so nothing reflows. 7 DATE — inline-start void; weekday 11px muted above, day numeral Kufam 700 at 64px, month and year 15px below. Western digits via ar-EG-u-nu-latn. 8 TIME — shares the date's void, 15px, one bobbin between. 9 VENUE — centre void, name at 19px ink, Maps button a 44px-tall accent-outlined pill inside the void, never over lattice. 10 COUNTDOWN — a wide 170px void, four cells side by side, numerals Kufam 700 at 34px, labels 10px muted. 11 MESSAGE — inline-end void, 16px, capped at 400 characters. 12 FOOTER — a small final void, 11px muted, one bobbin run above it.


**Motion.** SIGNATURE (scroll-linked): the lattice ground is a position: fixed full-viewport layer translated on Y at 0.35× scroll progress via useScroll → useTransform → translate3d. Because the screen moves slower than the content, the two planes visibly separate in depth as you descend — the card sits behind the window. One composited transform on one promoted layer, no paint, no filter. Reveal-time: each void's rim strokes on once at 240ms as it enters, opacity only. Nothing loops. prefers-reduced-motion: the lattice is static and that static screen is the finished state.


**Confetti.** colours ['#6f3d16', '#c8a06a', '#ebe1cc', '#4a2f14', '#f7f1e4'] · shapes ['strip', 'square'] · count 110. Sawn-wood offcuts — a lathe throws strips and squares, never petals. — **Music.** `qanun-serenade`


**Differentiators.** Content sits in HOLES, not on a ground. No other theme in the set — new or existing — puts its blocks inside voids cut through a full-bleed pattern; classic puts a panel on paper, modern puts a column on air, this puts nothing anywhere and cuts windows instead.; The only theme whose closed cover is genuinely opaque. Every other cover shows you the card and animates it in; this one hides the card behind a screen and then makes a window.; Highest ground density of any light theme in the set (a full-bleed lattice at 8%) — the answer for segment B, who reads air as unfinished, without going dark or saturated.; Consecutive blocks alternate inline-start / centre / inline-end, so there is no vertical axis at all — the exact opposite of midnight's centred column and of الإيوان's framed one.; Pale ground with a deep walnut lattice, not warm stone with a brick arch: at picker thumbnail size this is a woven grey-gold screen and الإيوان is a red-brown doorway.


**Pitfalls.** MOIRÉ: an 8% lattice tile behind a 0.35× parallax layer is the exact interference pair that aliases on mid-range Android. The tile must be authored at an even pixel size (22px, never scaled) and the parallax layer must be transform-only. Test at DPR 2.75 before the pattern pitch is fixed.; THE COVER SILHOUETTE MUST NOT DEPEND ON A WEBFONT. If the 12% names layer waits for Kufam to load, the closed card is a blank screen on a slow WhatsApp webview and the uncle closes it. Render it in the fallback stack and let it swap.; THE MEASURE IS 244px, NOT 300px. Text laid inside a hexagon wastes its corners. Author every size from the four-line long-name case inside a 244px measure at 390px before anything else is drawn.; Do not build the reveal by animating an SVG <mask> element — it repaints the whole masked layer each frame. CSS mask-image plus transform: scale on one promoted layer.


### الإيوان — Iwan (`iwan`) — **BUILT**

**Segment.** B primary (world-building Cairo bride), A secondary. زفاف, the formal hotel-ballroom register. Top of the light-ground ladder.

**Pitch.** The whole page stands inside one great Cairene arch — you read the invitation from within the hall, and the walls narrow around you as you walk in.

**Palette.** bg `#f3ece1` · panel `#e9dfcd` · ink `#2a231c` · muted `#5f5341` · accent `#7b3f2e` · accentSoft `#c69a86` · line `rgba(123, 63, 46, 0.24)` · themeColor `#f3ece1`

**Contrast.** ink 13.20:1 on bg / 11.73:1 on panel; muted 6.39 / 5.68; accent 6.90 / 6.13. accentSoft 2.14 / 1.90 — voussoirs, rims and ornament only, never text. FIX GRAFTED: muted darkened from #665847 to #5f5341, taking panel headroom from 5.21 to 5.68, because the tapering measure forces body text into narrower columns where every point of contrast is worth more.

**Fonts.** AR Qahiri / Fustat — EN Forum / Alegreya Sans. Weights: Qahiri 400 — single weight, display-only, never below 36px. Fustat 400 and 600. Amiri Quran 400, per-theme tokens: Bismillah 2.5rem, verse 1.0625rem / 2.0. Forum 400 (single weight). Alegreya Sans 400 and 500.

> Qahiri is a display Kufi drawn from Cairo's own monumental lettering — its reference is literally the inscription band over a Cairo portal, which is exactly what this theme is. That is provenance we can print in the product copy without reaching for mysticism. Fustat is a flat, unfussy modern Arabic named for the first city on this site; it does not compete with the Kufi, which is the whole job of a body face here. Forum is a brushed Roman capital that behaves like engraved Latin — the same act performed in the other script — and Alegreya Sans matches Fustat's colour so the English body is warm rather than corporate.


**Structure.** FRAME-AS-MEASURE — the axis this theme owns. The arch is not decoration over a column; it IS the column. A single horseshoe crown sits at the top of the first viewport, and two legs — 2px accent strokes — run the entire scroll height down both inline margins to a 6px stepped base at the footer. Every content block lives inside those legs, so the reading measure is defined by the arch and by nothing else: no max-width token exists in this theme. At three points the legs step inward by 9px each (a haunch) and the measure narrows with them, so the column visibly tapers as you descend — 342px at the crown, 315px, 296px, 286px at the base. Total taper is capped at 28px of inset regardless of page height; the measure may never drop below 286px. The crown holds the names, the springing zone holds the Bismillah and verse, the shaft holds everything else. No panel, no cards, no dividers, no horizontal rules — the arch replaces all of them.


**Ornament.** One bin: Cairene stone, three scales. (1) THE HORSESHOE ARCH — viewBox 0 0 342 240; a path rising vertically from each springing point, curving inward past the vertical at the haunch (the horseshoe overhang), meeting at a crown. Nine voussoirs are drawn as radial trapezoids across the arch head, ABLAQ-treated: alternating voussoirs filled accent at 0.18, the others left empty. (2) THE MUQARNAS CLUSTER — four stepped cells at the apex, each a small trapezoid with a chamfered lower edge, drawn 1px in accent; and at 190px an eleven-cell three-tier half-dome for the no-photo state. (3) THE ABLAQ COURSE — alternating 14px bands. HERO: the 240px crown itself. CONNECTIVE: a 24px three-voussoir stub, three small trapezoids alternating fill, laid across the measure where a divider would go. GROUND: a repeating-linear-gradient at 0deg alternating 14px transparent with 14px rgba(123,63,46,0.055), applied to --inv-panel zones only, never behind body text. All symmetric about the vertical axis — RTL costs nothing.


**Cover.** CLOSED: the portal seen straight on and shut. The arch drawn in full at 88% of the viewport width, its opening filled solid --inv-panel carrying the ablaq courses, the couple's names cut into the crown in Qahiri, the four-line block sitting on the springing line. The open control is a solid accent bar across the base of the arch. OPEN: the door opens. The panel fill inside the arch splits down the centre into two leaves; each rotates outward about its own outer leg with perspective: 900px and transform-style: preserve-3d, 0 → ∓78°, 700ms ease-out — you see them swing inward, not fade. Simultaneously the four muqarnas cells at the apex drop 4px in sequence at 90ms stagger and settle. Behind the leaves the card is already painted. The arch itself stays and becomes the first frame of the shaft. Reduced motion: leaves are open at rest, cross-fade.


**Card.** 1 NAMES — under the crown, centred, Qahiri 400 at 56px on the 342px measure, four lines. Long-name case: عبد الرحمن و ياسمين takes three lines at 56px inside 342px; the crown's height grows with the block, the type does not shrink. 2 BISMILLAH + VERSE — in the springing zone, on --inv-panel with the ablaq tile suppressed to 0 behind it. Amiri Quran, Bismillah 2.5rem centred, verse 1.0625rem / 2.0, source 11px muted. Never on the pattern. 3 POETRY — first block of the shaft, 18px Fustat 400 muted, centred, a three-voussoir stub above. 4 INVITATION LINE — 17px ink, full measure. 5 ROLES — two stacked rows, label 11px muted (word-spacing 0.3em, no letter-spacing), name 20px Fustat 600, a voussoir stub between them. Measure has stepped once by here: 315px. 6 PHOTO — masked to a smaller horseshoe arch, the same path at 0.4 scale, 2px accent voussoirs around its head, set into the shaft as a window in the hall wall. NO PHOTO: that position takes the 190px muqarnas half-dome — eleven stepped cells in three tiers, accent strokes on --inv-panel. This is the strongest object in the theme and the picker should show it, so the photo-less card is the flagship. 7 DATE — measure 296px; weekday 11px muted, day numeral Qahiri 400 at 68px, month and year 16px. Western digits via ar-EG-u-nu-latn. 8 TIME — 16px, beneath the date, one stub between. 9 VENUE — name 19px ink; Maps button a 44px accent-filled bar at the full current measure. 10 COUNTDOWN — measure 286px, four cells, numerals Fustat 600 at 32px, labels 10px muted. 11 MESSAGE — 16px, capped 400 characters. 12 FOOTER — 11px muted above the stepped base where the two legs terminate.


**Motion.** SIGNATURE (scroll-linked): the arch builds itself around the reader. The two legs are ONE SVG path with stroke-dasharray set to its own length; stroke-dashoffset is bound directly to scroll progress, throttled to whole percentage steps, so the arch extends just ahead of the thumb and reaches its stepped base exactly at the footer. One attribute on one isolated path, no filter, no vector-effect. Reveal-time: each block rises 6px once, 70ms stagger. Nothing loops. prefers-reduced-motion: legs fully drawn from first paint, doors already open.


**Confetti.** colours ['#7b3f2e', '#c69a86', '#f3ece1', '#e9dfcd', '#2a231c'] · shapes ['square', 'strip'] · count 130. Cut stone and mortar. No round shapes anywhere in this theme, including the confetti — the rule that keeps the bin coherent. — **Music.** `cinematic-forever`


**Differentiators.** The frame IS the layout. No other theme lets an ornament define the reading measure, and none other has a measure that changes as you scroll. مشربية cuts holes in a screen; this draws a wall on both sides of you and moves it inward.; The only tapering measure in the set. غروب's bands widen the page optically, حديقة's gutter holds the measure constant — this narrows it by 28px over the scroll, which is felt rather than seen.; A door that swings on two rotateY hinges, not a fade, a curtain or a dissolve. The gesture is architectural and it is the one every guest recognises from a real Cairo portal.; The muqarnas half-dome makes the NO-PHOTO card the better card. That inverts the usual apology and it is the state the picker should render.; Warm Cairene stone with a brick-red arch, ablaq courses, no gold anywhere — visibly Islamic architecture without the gold-on-cream register that anchors the bottom of every mature market.


**Pitfalls.** THE CROWN AND THE LEGS ARE TWO SVGs. A full-page arch stretched to an unknown scroll height distorts the horseshoe. The legs get preserveAspectRatio: none and are non-uniformly scaled; the crown is a separate fixed-aspect SVG. Getting this wrong is the theme's single build failure.; CAP THE TAPER AT 28px TOTAL. Three haunch steps on a long card can drive the final measure below 260px, at which point Arabic body text starts breaking badly. Cap it and hold, regardless of page height.; Qahiri is single-weight and display-only. It must never drop below 36px and the hierarchy has nowhere to come from but size and interval — prove the four-line fathers' block at 56px inside 342px before anything else is built.; The verse must sit on plain panel with the ablaq tile suppressed. Qur'anic text over a striped ground is the specific thing the house rules forbid.


### قنديل — Qandeel (`qandeel`) — spec only

**Segment.** B primary. زفاف, evening reception, the ceiling-installation bride. Top price tier. REPLACES MIDNIGHT — see differentiators.

**Pitch.** Everything on the card hangs from the top of the page on its own thread, at its own depth, like the lanterns strung over the hall.

**Palette.** bg `#0f1e22` · panel `#16292d` · ink `#f0e8dc` · muted `#b9ada0` · accent `#e3b566` · accentSoft `#6f8f8c` · line `rgba(227, 181, 102, 0.20)` · themeColor `#0f1e22`

**Contrast.** ink 14.06:1 on bg / 12.44:1 on panel; muted 7.77 / 6.87; accent 9.00 / 7.97. accentSoft 4.87 / 4.31 — passes on bg but not on panel, so it is declared ornament-only and may never carry text in either zone. No fix needed: the dark ground gives this theme the widest headroom of any palette in the set.

**Fonts.** AR Reem Kufi Ink / Almarai — EN Prata / Manrope. Weights: Reem Kufi Ink 400 and 600. Almarai 300, 400 and 700 — 300 is used only for the 15px captions, which need it on a dark ground. Amiri Quran 400, per-theme tokens: Bismillah 2.375rem, verse 1.125rem / 2.0 with a 24px minimum, placed on the NEAR layer where no parallax applies. Prata 400 (single weight). Manrope 400 and 600.

> Reem Kufi Ink is Reem Kufi with a deliberately inked, softly bled stroke. On a dark ground that bleed reads as glow spill around the letterform — an effect a clean Kufi cannot give and one this theme is entirely about. It shares a skeleton with midnight's plain Reem Kufi, which is fine precisely because this theme replaces midnight; the difference between the two cuts is legible at picker size. Almarai is slightly rounded and stays legible in cream on deep petrol at 15px. Prata is a high-contrast Latin display whose thin strokes catch the same light; Manrope keeps the English body from thinning out on the dark.


**Structure.** SUSPENSION AND Z-DEPTH — the axis this theme owns, and the only genuine third dimension in the set. (a) EVERYTHING HANGS. Every ornament and every content block is suspended from the top edge of its section on a 1px plumb line of a DIFFERENT length. The sequence is an authored, commented token array: 40, 96, 62, 130, 78, 54, 112, 68px. Nothing sits on a shared baseline; the vertical rhythm is deliberately irregular, which is what makes the page read as a room rather than a document. (b) THREE DEPTH PLANES. Elements are assigned to far / mid / near, which differ in scale (0.82 / 0.92 / 1.0), opacity (0.45 / 0.72 / 1.0) and colour temperature (the far layer tinted toward accentSoft petrol-green), and which parallax against each other on scroll. Content is ALWAYS on near and always at full legibility; far and mid carry ornament only. Hard budget: no more than six elements per background layer, enforced in the component, not in a comment. No panel, no card, no column border — the reading measure is 342px on near and nothing else is measured.

**Pitfalls.** DO NOT ANIMATE text-shadow FROM SCROLL. Cross-fade three pre-rendered aria-hidden bevel layers by opacity instead. Animated text-shadow on 3.25rem Arabic is a per-frame text repaint and will not hold 60fps on the target device.; ARABIC IS START-ALIGNED, NOT JUSTIFIED. CSS justifies Arabic by stretching word spaces and produces rivers immediately at 20rem. Justify Latin only, and document the divergence as a deliberate decision.; HARD-CAP THE CUSTOM MESSAGE AT 260 CHARACTERS in this theme's renderer. With no section breaks, a 300-word message is a wall of text and nothing rescues it.; The verse carries no bevel. Two text-shadows on large Arabic smear tashkeel on a low-DPR Android, and the verse is exactly where that is least acceptable.; The measure must not drop below 20rem. Below 360px viewport width, hold the measure and let the field inset shrink instead.

## Considered and dropped

- MASHRABIYA'S SIBLING, ARCH (تيار) — deep teal #0f2b2b with apricot #f0a06a, names knocked out of a solid apricot arch fill. Cut as a third arch concept behind الإيوان and طاج, but the PALETTE and the KNOCKOUT COVER are the best dark non-gold pairing anyone proposed, and the argument behind it — that a dark card does not have to be a gold card — is one the set currently cannot make, since both its darks are gold. Worth reviving as a colourway of الإيوان, which has a real arch structure to carry it.
- SHAMA / شمعة (8 market, 6 craft, 8 build) — the highest-scoring concept I cut, and cut purely on the parent's own rule: it is a dark, gold, evening, one-lit-object card for segment B, and قنديل is already that. Its travelling scroll-linked light pool is a genuinely good object and it builds cleanly (a single fixed spring-damped radial-gradient behind content). If the set ever grows to sixteen and a third dark is wanted, this is it — with the craft fix applied, an irregular off-centre pool rather than a clean ellipse, and the market fix, an unlit ground tone at which the next block is still faintly legible.
- MEEQAT / الميقات (7/7/7) — an off-axis rail with content hanging from labelled, TIMED nodes, extending naturally into a running order (استقبال ٨:٠٠، الزفة ٩:٣٠، العشاء ١٠:٣٠). The only concept that answers what a guest actually asks. Cut for two reasons: its scroll-drawn vertical line at an inline offset is حديقة's structural device, and the running-order content it depends on is not in the schema. SHIP THE RUNNING-ORDER FIELD FIRST — it is a real feature with a price attached — and then this theme, not the other way round.
- HINNA / حنة (8/7/6) — one continuous henna trail that is every divider on the card, drawn by scroll exactly as far as you have read, with the ornament and the reveal being the same gesture. The most physical scroll signature proposed. Cut because خيامية already owns the henna night and حديقة owns the drawn line. Its cover — the trail drawing forward into a paisley medallion with the names appearing inside as the line closes around them — is the best single gesture left on the table.
- TAYYA / طيّة (8/6/6) — four overlapping translucent vellum leaves where the alpha-multiply overlap band IS the divider. Elegant, and segment A would buy it. Cut on a defect of goods: at 0.82 alpha every one of its seven contrast tokens is notional, which breaks the colour contract rather than bending it. Revivable only if the composited leaf colour is computed once and entered into the token table as a real value.
- KHATM / ختم — folded into الظرف, which scored three points higher on build and places its creases by content boundary. What is worth carrying across separately is khatm's CORRESPONDENCE LIST treatment: label at the inline-start in small caps, value at the inline-end, joined by a 1px dotted leader. That is a better details treatment than a bordered panel and it belongs in الظرف's bottom flap.
- TAZKARA / التذكرة (6/7/7) — perforated coupons with LABEL-LED, LEFT-ALIGNED fields, and edge notches built from two positioned solid circles rather than clip-path. Cut because الظرف serves segment C better and because a ticket reads as club entry to exactly the generation being protected. But its details treatment is the most legible practical block anyone drew — a guest finds the venue in one second — and it should be borrowed by a warmer theme rather than lost.
- TIRAZ / الطراز (7/6/7) — Fatimid woven bands with a 10px selvedge whose running stitch advances on scroll. Cut only because خيامية and غروب already take the two band slots the set can carry. Its Cormorant Unicase argument — the only Latin face that mixes cap and lowercase forms at one height, which is exactly what a band inscription does — is the cleverest cross-script observation in the review and is worth stealing.
- QAMARIYYA / قمرية (7/6/7) — a fanlight casting a widening light trapezoid, so the measure grows as you descend: the exact inverse of الإيوان's taper. Cut as a second architectural-aperture theme. If ever built, swap Reem Kufi Fun for plain Reem Kufi and get the softening from the gypsum strut geometry instead of from a quirky letterform.
- MAMLUKI, DARJ, TAJ, TALLY, SHAREET (FILM), IMMOBILIA, SAMAA, MURAL — all scored 5–7 and all lost to a theme in the set that owns their structural slot: mamluki and tiraz to the band pair, taj to الإيوان, darj to الظرف, tally and shareet to the two-dark cap, immobilia and samaa to the fact that their structural ideas (a tapering frame, an off-axis spine) are already owned. Two specific components are worth salvaging regardless: TIMETABLE's split-flap countdown, wired to fire exactly once on reveal with a ref guard, and INDEX's leader rule built as a flex:1 middle element with logical padding — the one implementation that is correct in both directions.
